## A simple place to write

Resery Blog is a small collection of notes on software, ideas, and making things. The design stays out of the way so the words can remain at the center.

## What you will find here

Expect technical notes, longer reflections, and occasional video essays. Every post may have an English version, a Chinese version, or both.

### About translations

When a translation exists, the language switch in the top-right corner opens the matching version directly.

## Video support

Posts can include responsive local videos, Bilibili embeds, and other external players without changing the reading layout.
